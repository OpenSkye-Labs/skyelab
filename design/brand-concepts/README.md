# SkyeLab app icon studies

[Open the full-resolution comparison sheet](sheet.png) at 100% to judge the smallest marks. Each column shows a launcher at 1024, 128, 32 and 16 px, followed by 32 and 16 px monochrome sidebar glyphs. Both rows use the same assets on different surfaces. The first column uses the shipped Porcelain master, Grip at 32 px and mini-Bean at 16 px.

## Fairweather · Bean evolution

Bean hooks one paw over the left edge, with the familiar uneven cloud crown and tilted smile close enough to read across a crowded Dock. A white sticker rim and a storm-blue inner line (`#385F82`) separate the warm glaze (`#FFF4E4`) from a morning sky (`#3D83C7` to `#C8E3ED`). Two low cloud shelves leave the sky above Bean open. The asymmetric crop, round eyes on a slope and separate paw carry SkyeLab’s own gesture. In the sidebar the paw survives at 32 px, while a complete mini-Bean gives the face more room at 16 px. The README banner could keep Bean on the right with its blue sky extending behind the title. In onboarding, the paw could appear first and draw Bean up from the corner.

## Pocket Bean · Bean evolution

Bean peeks out of a project folder with one paw folded over its tab. The tilted face and scalloped crown preserve the family resemblance, while the diagonal folder lip gives the character a pose specific to SkyeLab. Cream porcelain (`#FFF2DC`), a blue-green outline (`#365F6D`) and a white rim sit between an aqua sky (`#98D9EA` to `#428FBA`) and a glazed teal pocket (`#74BDBA` to `#307878`). A diagonal cut separates cloud and folder in the sidebar glyph. The same folder lip could run beneath the README banner title and hold a project’s account badge in the onboarding hero when the user binds their first folder.

## Foldwing · New direction

A project folder folds into a small flying bird. Its raised wing keeps the folder’s stepped tab, a single crease exposes the periwinkle underside (`#8CA5CB`), and an ochre beak (`#D6A06B`) points into the open sky. Ivory paper (`#FFF2DE`) and a slate outline (`#465873`) sit against a sky that warms from blue (`#7C9FD6`) to apricot (`#F4CCAE`). The angular silhouette and side-facing eye give it a distinct profile. The sidebar mark keeps the wing crease as a transparent cutout. On the README banner the bird could fly toward the right margin, and the onboarding hero could show a folder folding into the bird as its account is selected.

## Review notes

**Fairweather is the recommended direction.** Bean’s tilted face and lower-left grip connect it immediately to the current launcher, and the blue tile gives the warm porcelain a clear edge on a dark desktop. Pocket Bean is the stronger choice if the icon needs to explain the product’s folder identity. Foldwing’s silhouette survives in monochrome, though its folder tab is easiest to recognize at larger sizes.

Each concept directory contains an editable 1024 px launcher master (`icon.svg`) and its resvg preview (`icon.png`). The 32 and 16 px color launchers use `launcher-small.svg`, with broader contours and a simpler sky. The standalone `mark-32.svg` and `mark-16.svg` use `currentColor` and transparent cutouts. Their matching PNGs show the default black ink. All artwork uses local vector paths and gradients.
