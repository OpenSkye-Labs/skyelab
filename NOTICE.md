# Notices

## Themes

SkyeLab's bundled theme families in `crates/skyelab-app/themes/` are generated by `scripts/gen-themes.py`. These take
their palettes from the projects below. Each is used under its license; a few colors are darkened or lightened so
text passes SkyeLab's contrast floor (the script prints which).

| Tokyo Night | [folke/tokyonight.nvim](https://github.com/folke/tokyonight.nvim), Day and Night, © Folke Lemaitre | Apache-2.0 |
